## About us

### Our story
In my first year of university I felt the need for an activity that would help me reconnect with my creative, playful side, which I had pushed down more and more as I grew up. That's how I found clay, without knowing how much it would change my path. Working with it, I felt calm and curious, and today it's the way that brings me closer to my inner child.

Over time I realised that clay modelling isn't only a chance to connect with yourself, but also with the people sitting at the table with you. When hands are busy, people start to talk differently: more honestly, more gently, closer to one another.

AVA started from a passion, but along the way I discovered that while the hands work, the mind settles and people allow themselves to relax. That's how AVA became a place of connection, community and presence, where we create mindfully.

As we wanted to grow, the team grew too, each of us helping our community develop.

## Group workshops
Once a month we gather around the clay, with a new theme each time. We work with our hands, we talk, we listen to each other. You don't need experience, just curiosity.

- **How it works:** monthly workshop, 2 hours, a different theme, sometimes in collaboration with other artists or entrepreneurs
- **Included:** materials, guidance, a drink / snack, and the option of firing your piece
- **Group:** 12 people max, to keep it intimate
- **Price:** from 185 lei per person (varies by theme)
- **Where:** Strada Elefterie 31, Cotroceni, Bucharest
- **Collaborations:** Are you an artist or entrepreneur who'd like to host a workshop with us? Write to us. AVA Art & Soul is a growing community and we'd love to have you among our collaborators.
- **Button:** "See upcoming workshops"

**FAQ**
- **Do I need experience?** No. Just bring your curiosity, we'll take care of the rest.
- **What should I wear?** Comfortable clothes you don't mind getting clay on. Don't worry, clay comes off clothes and skin easily with a little warm water.
- **When do I get my piece?** Pieces go through drying and firing, so you can collect them after 4–6 weeks, from the workshop venue.
- **What if I can't make it?** As a young business, every booked seat matters to us. Payments aren't refunded, but if you let us know at least 24 hours in advance, we'll keep you a seat at the next workshop.
- **Can I give a workshop as a gift?** Yes. You can buy a gift voucher on the site ◇, or write to us and we'll prepare one.

## For companies

- **What it helps with:** stress and fatigue · new or remote teams who don't know each other · communication difficulties · the need for a real break
- **Formats:** 3-hour clay workshop · half day with clay and wellbeing themes · monthly wellbeing programme · teambuilding on request
- **Possible themes:** managing stress · communication and trust · the team as a whole · what motivates me
- **Logistics:** at your office or partner venues · groups of 4 to 16 · we bring all materials · invoiced through our PFA
- **Facilitated by:** an accredited personal-development counsellor + the team
- **Proof:** ⟨COMING SOON⟩
- **Price:** on request, with a tailored quote
- **Quote form:** name, company, email, phone, number of people, preferred date, location, what you'd like your team to get out of it

**FAQ**
- **How many people can join?** Between 4 and 16. For larger teams we split the group or run several sessions.
- **Do you come to our office?** Yes, with all the materials. We can also work at partner venues or a space you choose.

- **Do you issue invoices?** Yes, we invoice the company.
- **How much does it cost?** Each workshop is designed for your team, so we send a tailored quote once we know what your team needs and which format you choose.

## 1:1 Counselling with Alessia Văideanu
I support you on your path and guide you towards your authentic self. I help you explore what's troubling you, reach the goals you set, and build healthy relationships with the people around you.

**Does this sound like you?**
- You're going through a change and don't yet know what place it has in your life?
- You feel stuck, even though you want to move forward?
- You want to know yourself better, to understand what you feel and why?
- You wonder where you want to go and what really matters to you?

- **How it works:** 60–90 minutes, online or in person, weekly, then fortnightly or monthly, depending on your needs
- **First meeting:** book a free 15-minute online intro call
- **Price:** 150 lei / hour
- **Counselling or psychotherapy?** Personal-development counselling does not replace psychotherapy or medical treatment. It is for people without mental health disorders. If along the way we feel you need specialised help, I'll tell you honestly and help you find the right specialist.
- **Confidentiality:** Everything we talk about stays between us.
- **Button:** "Book a session"

**FAQ**
- **How many sessions will I need?** It depends on you and what you want to work on. Some people come for a few meetings, others for a longer process. We decide together after the first session and adapt as we go.
- **Online or in person?** Both, whichever suits you.
- **Does what we discuss stay between us?** Yes. Everything you tell me is confidential.
- **What if I need psychotherapy?** If we feel you need a different kind of support, I'll tell you honestly and help you find the right specialist.
- **How do I pay?** Online, by card, when you book. ◇

## Private 1:1 workshop with Alessia Văideanu
The clay, you and me. A self-discovery workshop through modelling, at your own pace, no group, no rush. For adults or children.

**The private workshop is for you if:**
- You find it hard to put what you feel into words?
- You find it hard to open up to others, especially in a group?
- You feel uncomfortable being vulnerable, but still want to know yourself better?

**The private workshop is for your child if:**
- They find it hard to say what they feel or think?
- They need to develop fine or gross motor skills?
- They're working on coordination and need an activity they enjoy?
- They feel more at ease playing than talking?

Our workshops don't replace speech, occupational or physical therapy, but they can complement them beautifully.

- **Duration:** 90 minutes
- **Options:** for one person · for two (couples, mother–daughter) · as a gift voucher
- **Price:** 250 lei adult · 220 lei child · 450 lei for 2 — includes materials and firing
- **Button:** "Book a private workshop"

**FAQ**
- **Do I need to know how to model clay?** No. It's not about how pretty it turns out, but what you discover along the way.
- **From what age can children come?** From 5.
- **Can I stay with my child during the workshop?** We recommend leaving them on their own; at the end we'll tell you how it went.
- **Does the workshop replace the therapy my child needs?** Our workshops don't replace speech, occupational or physical therapy, but they can complement them beautifully.
- **Can I give a private workshop as a gift?** Yes, as a gift voucher bought on the site ◇, or write to us.
- **When do I get my piece?** After drying and firing, in about 4–6 weeks.
