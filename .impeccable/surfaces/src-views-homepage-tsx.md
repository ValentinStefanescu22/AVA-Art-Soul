# Home page (Acasă / Home)

Scope: the home page, RO `/` and EN `/en`. Visitor mode: Persuade.
Audience and job: individuals looking for a calm way back to themselves, and HR or team leads looking for a team workshop. Within 30 seconds they understand who AVA is, what it offers, and press "Vezi atelierele" or "Pentru companii". Proof on hand: real prices, the next event, the founder's training. No testimonials, gallery or retreat details yet: those blocks show "În curând".
Chosen by the client on 2026-10-17 from three mockups (design/directions/index.html, direction B).

## Direction contract
THESIS: The home page is an evening at the workshop table: warm light on clay in the dark, lifting into sand as you read. It refuses the category default of a cream page with a centred serif headline and a grid of service cards.
OWN-WORLD: Umber dusk (umber-900 to umber-700) under a full-bleed warm photo, then a dawn band into sand-200 and sand-100. Text on dusk uses the dusk tier (dusk-fg, dusk-fg-2, dusk-accent); on sand, ink and clay-600. Fraunces light for voice, Jost for information, Pinyon Script for a few words. Pill buttons with an arrow well. Woodsprite motes glow additively on dusk.
STORY: The visitor sees hands in clay and the promise "autocunoaștere prin creație", reads who the three friends are, browses five services as tall photos, learns the three steps of a workshop, then meets the next real event with its date first, and ends at the newsletter.
FIRST VIEWPORT: Full-bleed photo with umber veil; nav on top in dusk-fg; bottom-left: wordmark, tagline in Pinyon Script at clamp(3.2rem, 2rem + 6vw, 7rem), one lead sentence, primary pill "Vezi atelierele" (sand on dusk) and ghost pill "Pentru companii". Motes gather at the primary pill on hover.
FORM: Direction B "Amurg", second of the three client-pinned directions (concept seed 85af9e4f; the roll's challengers were declined, their disciplines kept as raises in the spec).
FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
